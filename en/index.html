FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is the Bible quiz free?

Yes. Bible Quiz International is free to use. Limited advertising may appear on selected learning and content pages to support the operation and continued development of the website. Ads are kept separate from quiz answers and essential navigation controls.

How many questions are included?

The library contains 5,000+ questions in English and Spanish, with shared IDs and references.

Which Bible books are covered?

The stated scope is the 66-book Bible: 39 Old Testament books and 27 New Testament books.

Is the website denominationally neutral?

Questions focus on people, places, events, structure and textual content, avoiding disputed interpretations presented as universal facts.

Is it suitable for children?

Yes. You can choose easy questions, turn off the timer and create local profiles for family members.
