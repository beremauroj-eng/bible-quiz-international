What you will learn

Locate cities, regions, rivers, seas, mountains and journeys across the biblical world. The collection combines direct questions with explanations and references to reinforce understanding rather than memory alone.

  • Major Old Testament regions and cities
  • Locations in the Gospels
  • Missionary journeys and cities in Acts

Sample questions and answers

According to the biblical account, which city was destroyed along with Gomorrah?
Answer: Sodom
Reference: Genesis 19:24–29

Sodom and Gomorrah were judged and destroyed.

Bible fact check: How many plagues struck Egypt before the exodus?
Answer: Ten
Reference: Exodus 7–12

Exodus records ten plagues upon Egypt.

Which city’s walls fell after Israel marched around it?
Answer: Jericho
Reference: Joshua 6:1–20

The walls of Jericho fell after Israel followed God’s instructions.

Which prophet named Bethlehem as the place from which a ruler would come?
Answer: Micah
Reference: Micah 5:2

Micah foretold a ruler coming from Bethlehem.

How to use this topic

Answer first without consulting a prompt. Then open the explanation, verify the reference and note any names, places or sequences you did not remember. Repeat the topic later and compare your accuracy and response time.

Editorial approach

Questions are written to describe biblical content and avoid presenting a disputed denominational interpretation as a universal fact.
