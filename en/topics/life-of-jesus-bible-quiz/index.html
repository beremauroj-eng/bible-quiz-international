Answer: Moab

How to use this topic

Answer first without consulting a prompt. Then open the explanation, verify the reference and note any names, places or sequences you did not remember. Repeat the topic later and compare your accuracy and response time.

Editorial approach

Questions are written to describe biblical content and avoid presenting a disputed denominational interpretation as a universal fact.
