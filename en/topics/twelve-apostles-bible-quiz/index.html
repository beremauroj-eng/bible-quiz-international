What you will learn

Questions about the disciples Jesus called, their names, experiences and roles in the Gospels and Acts. The collection combines direct questions with explanations and references to reinforce understanding rather than memory alone.

  • Names and relationships among the Twelve
  • Key moments in the Gospels
  • Witness and leadership in Acts

Sample questions and answers

What was the first sign Jesus performed as recorded in John’s Gospel?
Answer: Turning water into wine
Reference: John 2:1–11

John identifies the transformation of water into wine at Cana as the first of Jesus’ signs.

In which town was Jesus born?
Answer: Bethlehem
Reference: Matthew 2:1; Luke 2:4–7

Jesus was born in Bethlehem during the reign of Herod.

Who baptised Jesus in the Jordan River?
Answer: John the Baptist
Reference: Matthew 3:13–17

John the Baptist baptised Jesus, after which the Spirit descended like a dove.

How many apostles did Jesus choose?
Answer: 12
Reference: Mark 3:13–19

Jesus appointed twelve apostles to be with Him and to be sent out to preach.

How to use this topic

Answer first without consulting a prompt. Then open the explanation, verify the reference and note any names, places or sequences you did not remember. Repeat the topic later and compare your accuracy and response time.

Editorial approach

Questions are written to describe biblical content and avoid presenting a disputed denominational interpretation as a universal fact.
