Privacy

Your learning data stays in your browser

No account is required. This policy explains local quiz data, hosting, cookies, consent, advertising and your privacy choices.

Last updated: 2 August 2026

Bible Quiz International is committed to providing a respectful, transparent and privacy-conscious Bible learning experience.

1. Information stored on your device

The website may store quiz settings, local profiles, progress, history, achievements, bookmarks and preferences in your browser’s local storage. This information normally remains on your device and is not transmitted to Bible Quiz International.

You can remove locally stored information through your browser settings or through any backup, reset or data-management tools provided by the website.

2. Information transmitted when using the website

When you visit the website, standard technical information may be processed by our hosting, security and advertising providers. This may include:

  • IP address
  • Browser and device type
  • Operating system
  • Requested pages
  • Date and time of access
  • General location derived from an IP address
  • Cookie, consent or advertising identifiers
  • Security and diagnostic information

Cloudflare provides website hosting, content delivery, security and related infrastructure services.

3. Advertising

Bible Quiz International may use Google AdSense to display advertising on selected pages.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies, web beacons, device identifiers or similar technologies to serve, measure and personalise advertising where permitted.

Google’s use of advertising cookies may allow it and its partners to serve ads based on a visitor’s previous visits to this website or other websites.

Advertising is not intended to affect quiz answers, educational content or editorial decisions.

4. Personalised and non-personalised advertising

Depending on your location, consent choices and applicable law, advertising may be:

  • Personalised using previous activity or inferred interests
  • Non-personalised using contextual information such as the current page
  • Limited where consent or required signals are unavailable

Visitors may be shown a privacy or consent message before advertising cookies or personalised advertising are used.

5. Consent choices

Visitors in applicable regions may be asked to consent to the use of cookies, local storage and personal data for advertising and measurement.

Where available, visitors can consent, decline consent, manage individual options, and change or withdraw their choices later.

A privacy choices or consent-settings link may be provided in the website footer or through the consent message.

6. Google advertising information

You can learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services through Google’s privacy and advertising information pages.

You may also be able to manage advertising personalisation through your Google advertising settings and browser controls.

7. Children and families

Bible Quiz International includes content suitable for families and younger learners. We do not knowingly require children to create accounts or submit personal information to use the quiz.

Advertising may be restricted or excluded from pages specifically designed for children. Where required, age-appropriate advertising treatment or non-personalised advertising controls may be applied.

Parents, guardians and educators should supervise children’s use of the website where appropriate.

8. External links and advertisements

The website may contain links to third-party websites or advertisements operated by external organisations. Bible Quiz International does not control the privacy practices, content or security of those external services.

Visitors should review the privacy policies of any third-party website they choose to visit.

9. Data retention

Locally stored quiz information remains on the visitor’s device until it is cleared by the visitor, removed through browser settings or deleted using the website’s data-management tools.

Information processed by hosting, security, consent and advertising providers is retained according to those providers’ respective policies and applicable legal requirements.

10. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or withdrawal of consent.

Because most quiz information is stored locally, you can usually control or delete it directly through your browser.

11. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website, advertising services, legal requirements or privacy practices change. The updated date will appear at the top of this page.

12. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be submitted through the Contact page.
