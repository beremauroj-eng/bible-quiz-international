Privacy

Your learning data stays in your browser

This version requires no account and uses no advertising or third-party analytics.

Data stored locally

Settings, profiles, history, achievements and bookmarks are saved in browser storage.

Data transfer

The website does not transmit quiz progress to a server. Data is exported only when you deliberately create a backup or share a result.

Cookies and analytics

The delivered package includes no advertising, marketing cookies or third-party analytics scripts. If you add analytics during deployment, update this policy and apply any consent required by applicable law.

User control

You can clear site data through browser settings or use the website’s backup and restore tools.
