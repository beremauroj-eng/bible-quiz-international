How to study Judges

When studying Judges, begin with the book’s structure and the immediate context of each passage. Place the material in Canaan and within the period described as period of the judges. Then compare recurring people, locations, speeches and events. The quiz uses short explanations and references so you can return to the text before accepting an answer.
