In which testament is the book of Numbers found?
Answer: Old Testament
Reference: Numbers

Numbers is part of the Old Testament.

How many spies were sent to explore Canaan?
Answer: Twelve
Reference: Numbers 13:1–16

One leader from each tribe was sent to explore the land.

Which two spies gave a faithful report about entering Canaan?
Answer: Joshua and Caleb
Reference: Numbers 14:6–9

Joshua and Caleb trusted that God could give Israel the land.

How to study Numbers

When studying Numbers, begin with the book’s structure and the immediate context of each passage. Place the material in Sinai to Moab and within the period described as wilderness journey. Then compare recurring people, locations, speeches and events. The quiz uses short explanations and references so you can return to the text before accepting an answer.

Historical and geographical context

Period: Wilderness journey. Setting: Sinai to Moab. These labels provide broad orientation and do not replace close study of each passage.
