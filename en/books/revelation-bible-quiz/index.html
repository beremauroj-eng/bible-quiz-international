Sample questions and answers

In which testament is the book of Revelation found?
Answer: New Testament
Reference: Revelation

Revelation is part of the New Testament.

Which church in Revelation was described as lukewarm?
Answer: Laodicea
Reference: Revelation 3:14–22

The message to Laodicea rebukes the church for being neither cold nor hot.

On which island did John receive the visions recorded in Revelation?
Answer: Patmos
Reference: Revelation 1:9

John states that he was on the island of Patmos because of the word of God and testimony of Jesus.

How to study Revelation

When studying Revelation, begin with the book’s structure and the immediate context of each passage. Place the material in Patmos and Asia Minor and within the period described as apocalyptic vision. Then compare recurring people, locations, speeches and events. The quiz uses short explanations and references so you can return to the text before accepting an answer.

Historical and geographical context

Period: Apocalyptic vision. Setting: Patmos and Asia Minor. These labels provide broad orientation and do not replace close study of each passage.
