How to study Zephaniah

When studying Zephaniah, begin with the book’s structure and the immediate context of each passage. Place the material in Judah and Jerusalem and within the period described as reign of josiah. Then compare recurring people, locations, speeches and events. The quiz uses short explanations and references so you can return to the text before accepting an answer.

Historical and geographical context

Period: Reign of Josiah. Setting: Judah and Jerusalem. These labels provide broad orientation and do not replace close study of each passage.
