What is Genesis about?

Origins, covenant promises and the family narratives of Abraham, Isaac, Jacob and Joseph.

This page brings together selected questions about Genesis, with context, references and links for further study. The aim is not to impose a doctrinal interpretation, but to help you recognise what appears in the text and verify each answer in its cited passage.

According to the biblical account, which city was destroyed along with Gomorrah?
Answer: Sodom

Answer: Israel
Reference: Genesis 32:28

God changed Jacob’s name to Israel.

Who killed his brother Abel?
Answer: Cain
Reference: Genesis 4:8

Cain killed Abel in the field.

How to study Genesis

When studying Genesis, begin with the book’s structure and the immediate context of each passage. Place the material in Ancient Near East and within the period described as beginnings and the patriarchs. Then compare recurring people, locations, speeches and events. The quiz uses short explanations and references so you can return to the text before accepting an answer.

Historical and geographical context

Period: Beginnings and the patriarchs. Setting: Ancient Near East. These labels provide broad orientation and do not replace close study of each passage.
