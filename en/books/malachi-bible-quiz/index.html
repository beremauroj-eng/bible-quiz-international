How to study Malachi

When studying Malachi, begin with the book’s structure and the immediate context of each passage. Place the material in Judah and within the period described as post-exilic community. Then compare recurring people, locations, speeches and events. The quiz uses short explanations and references so you can return to the text before accepting an answer.

Historical and geographical context

Period: Post-exilic community. Setting: Judah. These labels provide broad orientation and do not replace close study of each passage.
