Answer: Ten

Answer: Moses

What food appeared on the ground for Israel in the wilderness?
Answer: Manna
Reference: Exodus 16:13–15

God provided manna for Israel in the wilderness.

How to study Exodus

When studying Exodus, begin with the book’s structure and the immediate context of each passage. Place the material in Egypt and Sinai and within the period described as exodus and wilderness. Then compare recurring people, locations, speeches and events. The quiz uses short explanations and references so you can return to the text before accepting an answer.

Historical and geographical context

Period: Exodus and wilderness. Setting: Egypt and Sinai. These labels provide broad orientation and do not replace close study of each passage.
