How to study Daniel

When studying Daniel, begin with the book’s structure and the immediate context of each passage. Place the material in Babylon and Persia and within the period described as exile and imperial courts. Then compare recurring people, locations, speeches and events. The quiz uses short explanations and references so you can return to the text before accepting an answer.

Historical and geographical context

Period: Exile and imperial courts. Setting: Babylon and Persia. These labels provide broad orientation and do not replace close study of each passage.
