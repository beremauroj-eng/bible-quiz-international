How to study Jonah

When studying Jonah, begin with the book’s structure and the immediate context of each passage. Place the material in Mediterranean and Nineveh and within the period described as prophetic narrative. Then compare recurring people, locations, speeches and events. The quiz uses short explanations and references so you can return to the text before accepting an answer.

Period: Prophetic narrative. Setting: Mediterranean and Nineveh. These labels provide broad orientation and do not replace close study of each passage.
