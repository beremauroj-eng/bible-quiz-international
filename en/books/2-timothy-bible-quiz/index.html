How to study 2 Timothy

When studying 2 Timothy, begin with the book’s structure and the immediate context of each passage. Place the material in Roman imprisonment setting and within the period described as pastoral letters. Then compare recurring people, locations, speeches and events. The quiz uses short explanations and references so you can return to the text before accepting an answer.

Historical and geographical context

Period: Pastoral letters. Setting: Roman imprisonment setting. These labels provide broad orientation and do not replace close study of each passage.
