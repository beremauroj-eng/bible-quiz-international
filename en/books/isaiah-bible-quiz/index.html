How to study Isaiah

When studying Isaiah, begin with the book’s structure and the immediate context of each passage. Place the material in Judah and Jerusalem and within the period described as eighth-century prophetic ministry. Then compare recurring people, locations, speeches and events. The quiz uses short explanations and references so you can return to the text before accepting an answer.

Historical and geographical context

Period: Eighth-century prophetic ministry. Setting: Judah and Jerusalem. These labels provide broad orientation and do not replace close study of each passage.
