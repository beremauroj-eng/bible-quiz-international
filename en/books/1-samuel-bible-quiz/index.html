Answer: Hannah
Reference: 1 Samuel 1:9–28

Hannah prayed for a son and dedicated Samuel to God.

How to study 1 Samuel

When studying 1 Samuel, begin with the book’s structure and the immediate context of each passage. Place the material in Israel and within the period described as rise of the monarchy. Then compare recurring people, locations, speeches and events. The quiz uses short explanations and references so you can return to the text before accepting an answer.
