How to study Acts

When studying Acts, begin with the book’s structure and the immediate context of each passage. Place the material in Jerusalem to Rome and within the period described as early church and mission. Then compare recurring people, locations, speeches and events. The quiz uses short explanations and references so you can return to the text before accepting an answer.

Historical and geographical context

Period: Early church and mission. Setting: Jerusalem to Rome. These labels provide broad orientation and do not replace close study of each passage.
