Bible Quiz International

Accessible, carefully structured Bible learning

Bible Quiz International combines play, study and progress tracking in one bilingual experience.

Our purpose

The website helps individuals, families, groups and educators remember and better understand the Bible’s people, places, books, events and teachings.

Editorial scope

The library expressly states that it uses the 66-book order. Questions aim to be educational, respectful, denominationally neutral and clear when a statement depends on the chosen scope.

How the experience is built

Question IDs match across English and Spanish. Each question includes options, a correct answer, explanation, reference, difficulty, book and category. Learning tools add timelines, maps, collections, analytics and missed-question practice.

Privacy

Quiz progress is stored locally in the browser. Limited advertising may appear on selected content pages to support the operation and continued development of Bible Quiz International. Advertising and related consent technologies are explained in our Privacy Policy.
